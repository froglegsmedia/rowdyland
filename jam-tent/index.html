Rowdyland Zone

Welcome to The Jam Tent

Songwriters by day, a rowdy rave by night. Bring your guitar and your dancing boots.

Come on in

Daytime Songwriter Showcase

Acoustic sets all afternoon. Just a stool, a mic and the songs. Pull up a hay bale and hear them how they were written.

See the lineup ♫

Open Jam Circle

Bring a fiddle, a banjo or just a good voice. Everybody plays, nobody judges, and the circle keeps going as long as the songs do.

Mud Pit Bull Ride

A mechanical bull right in the middle of a mud pit. Hold on with one hand, wave with the other, and don't wear your good boots.

Late Night DJ Rave

After hours the lights go red, the bass drops and the tent gets rowdy. Dance close, stay late and get a little wild till 3 AM.

Play my song ♫

Tent Bar

Cold beer on tap, longnecks on ice and mason jar cocktails poured all night.

See the menu 🍺

Contests

Tug of war by the mud pit, pool tables and a dartboard that never gets a break. Pick a partner, call your shot and settle it in the tent.
