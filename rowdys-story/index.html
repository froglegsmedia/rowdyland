The legend

Rowdy's Story

How a small, timid horse found his stride and ran every horse home to Rowdyland.

Saddle up ↓

Chapter One

The Wild Land

Out in the wild land, horses race each other just for the thrill of it. No jockeys. No tack. No whips. Just hooves and dust. A small, timid colt named Rowdy wanted in, but the herd laughed him off. Too small. Too timid. Not fast enough.

Chapter Two

Finding the Stride

So Rowdy ran alone. Every day, all day, sprinting the open field, chasing a speed he could never catch. Then one day, mad as a hornet and twice as stubborn, he locked into a stride that made a rhythm. A beat. Rowdy Hop music was born, and pure want carried him faster than any horse had ever gone.

Chapter Three

The Night of the Ancestors

He ran into the sunset and kept going until the stars came out. That night the ancestors looked down from the sky and told him he would be the fastest horse of all time. They gave him his name. Rowdy.

Chapter Four

Pony Tale

Then he saw her. A white horse on the moonlit horizon. Pony Tale. He chased her clear across the desert to the oasis where she lived alone. Love at first sight. She was every bit as fast as him, and when they ran together the music went wild. Rowdy never went home that night.

Chapter Five

Captured

The racing industry rounds up wild horses and trains them to race. They took Rowdy. He kicked, he bucked, he did not belong. Some horses there were rich, cocky star athletes. Most were trapped. The only way out was to beat every one of them, so he did, and became the number one racehorse in the land. But deep in the racing years, Rowdy accepted his fate. The capture changed his heart and his spirit. At night he stood at the fence and mourned the wild horse he used to be.

Chapter Six

The Plan

One night Pony Tale found him. She had come to bust him out. Rowdy told her he was not the horse she fell for anymore. She told him that was okay. He had not lost himself. He had become better, and he was going to help so many others. Nose to nose by lantern light, the two of them cooked up a plan.

Chapter Seven

The Fire Horse

Rowdy dug deeper than he ever had. He caught fire. He became the Fire Horse, went for the Triple Crown gold, and took it.

Chapter Eight

Free

Then he busted down the gates and kicked down the walls. Every horse ran free, and they all ran home to Rowdyland to live and roam free forever.
