The legend

Rowdy's Story

How a small, timid horse found his stride and ran every horse home to Rowdyland.

Saddle up ↓

Chapter One

The Wild Land

Out in the wild land, horses race each other just for the thrill of it. No jockeys. No tack. No whips. Just hooves and dust. A small, timid chestnut colt named Rowdy wanted in, but the herd laughed him off. Too small. Too timid. Not fast enough.

Chapter Two

Finding the Stride

So Rowdy ran alone. Every day, all day, sprinting the open field, chasing a speed he could never catch. Then one day, mad as a hornet and twice as stubborn, he locked into a stride that made a rhythm. A beat. Rowdy Hop music was born, and pure want carried him faster than any horse had ever gone.

Chapter Three

The Night of the Ancestors

He ran into the sunset and kept going until the stars came out. That night the ancestors looked down from the sky and told him he would be the fastest horse of all time. They gave him his name. Rowdy.

Chapter Four

Pony Tale

Then he saw her. A white horse on the moonlit horizon. Pony Tale. He chased her clear across the desert to the oasis where she lived alone. Love at first sight. She was every bit as fast as him, and when they ran together the music went wild. Rowdy never went home that night.

Chapter Five

Captured

Then one night, the racing industry came for Rowdy. They roped him, hauled him off and locked him in a stall to be trained for the track. He kicked, he bucked, he did not belong. Some horses there were rich, cocky star athletes. Most were trapped. The only way out was to beat every one of them, so he did, and became the number one racehorse in the land. But deep in the racing years, Rowdy accepted his fate. At night he could still hear the wild herd's beat, but his own stride had gone quiet. He did not recognize the horse with a number on his back staring up from the water trough. He mourned the wild horse he used to be.

Chapter Six

The Plan

Pony Tale came all that way to find him, but she could not get near him in the stables. So she got herself captured on purpose, won over a trainer and raced her way all the way to the Triple Crown to find him. Racing him was the only way to get next to him. So she lined up beside him and beat him by a nose, stealing a win from the number one horse in the land. Rowdy told her he was not the horse she fell for anymore. She told him that was okay. He had become better, and he was going to help so many others. Then she told him her plan to get them both out.

Chapter Seven

Taken

The night before the final race of the Triple Crown, the stable hands caught Pony Tale out of her stall and punished her for it. She kept her cool. The next morning they punished her again and took the whip to her. Pony Tale had a short fuse, and that was it. She bucked and kicked the owner, and the judges disqualified her on the spot. Then Rowdy heard them say it. Take her to the slaughterhouse. She is no use to us anymore. He watched them load her up from behind a locked gate, just minutes before the biggest race of his life. There was no more long game. No more waiting. Rowdy was fired up, and there was no time left.

Chapter Eight

The Fire Horse

Then it was time for the final race of the Triple Crown. The gates opened and Rowdy ran on pure determination, every stride pulling him closer to Pony Tale. Halfway around the track his stride locked in to a new level, harder and faster than the day he first found the beat, and he caught fire. The flames burned away the chestnut colt and left him gold, with a mane and legs as red as the fire itself. He became the Fire Horse. He took the Triple Crown gold, and he never stopped running. He went straight on to rescue Pony Tale.

Chapter Nine

Free

Then he busted down the gates and kicked down the walls. Every horse ran free. Rowdy and Pony Tale led them all across the desert to a western oasis, and together they built Rowdyland. Now the festival happens there every year, and Rowdy and Pony Tale live happily ever after.
