What it means

A Perfect Place

Rowdyland is the perfect place where everyone is happy and loves each other. It is a western oasis for wild horses and wild hearts. A hometown waiting at the end of the road. Dreams come true here. Hands find hands here. And once you have been, Rowdyland lives in your heart forever.

The legend

How Rowdy Rose

Rowdy was a small, timid wild horse. The herd told him he was too small and too slow. So he trained alone, day after day, until he locked into a stride that made a rhythm. That beat became Rowdy Hop. He ran into the night of the ancestors, found love with Pony Tale, got captured by the racing industry, and rose again. Halfway around the Triple Crown track he caught fire. The flames burned the old horse away and left him gold, with a red mane and red legs. He became the Fire Horse. Then he busted every gate and led every horse home to build Rowdyland.

Every festival

The Fire Horse Ceremony

On the final night, just before the headliner, the grounds gather at Fire Horse Plaza. Notes you wrote all weekend become the spark. Rowdy the statue goes ablaze to the Rowdyland anthem, and when the fire burns out he stands there shining in gold. The ceremony is a tribute to our ancestors, the ones who looked down from the sky and named him Rowdy. Throw what you are done carrying into the fire and walk out new. Rowdy comes out gold, and so do we.

Renewal and acceptance

Shed Your Skin

Rowdyland stands for rising after the hard stuff. For becoming your best self. For shedding your old skin and coming out golden. Nobody here is perfect. Everybody here is rising. You are accepted as you are, and you are invited to become who you are meant to be. That is the heart of this place.

The people

We Are the Rowdies

Rowdies is the night club, and the Rowdies are us. Every self-declared rowdy who ever got knocked down and decided to get back up. Together we hold the line for love, for freedom, and for each other. If Rowdyland lives in your heart, you are already one of us. We are the Rowdies.
